Rules & Controls

🎯 The Objective

Fit all provided geometric pieces into the wooden frame so they form a 100% solid pattern without any overlaps and with no piece protruding past the boundary.

🎮 Controls

  • Drag & Drop: Click/touch and move pieces into the frame or back to the palette. Pieces magnetically snap to the frame grid.
  • Rotate (90°): Right-click on any piece, click the circular ↻ badge, or press R / Space.
  • Counter-Clockwise: Press Q.
  • Flip Horizontal: Press F to mirror asymmetric shapes (trapezia & triangles).
  • Return to Tray: Press Del or Backspace while a piece is selected.

⭐ Time-Based Star Ratings

  • ★★★ Grandmaster: Complete the packing before the first speed cutoff.
  • ★★☆ Expert: Complete before the second speed cutoff.
  • ★☆☆ Completed: Pack all pieces before the countdown timer hits 0.
